1. How to use this document with a design tool

AI design tools tend to invent features, screens and flows when a prompt is vague. To stop that:

  1. Attach this PDF, or paste the section for the screens you're designing, and tell the tool that this is the source of truth for features and content.
  2. Design one role at a time (Parent, then Doctor, then Pharmacist, then Owner), and ideally one screen or flow at a time. Each screen below lists its purpose, content, actions and states.
  3. Say what may change: colours, type, spacing, illustration, iconography, card styles, motion and layout details inside a screen.
  4. Say what must not change: the list of screens and tabs, the steps in each flow, the data shown, the button labels for key actions, and the rules in section 3.
  5. Ask for every state: loading, empty, error and success, plus the special states listed per screen (for example the token screen's six statuses).
  6. Check the output against section 12 ("Do not add"). Common inventions to reject: estimated wait times, chat, online payment, doctor ratings, symptom pickers, and cancel or reschedule buttons for parents.
Words that matter. The product speaks to parents about their children. Prefer "child" over "patient" in new copy. (A few current strings still say "patients ahead of you" and "Patient history"; a redesign may keep or fix those.) Currency is Indian rupees (₹), locale en-IN, timezone Asia/Kolkata, English only.

Section 13 has ready-made prompts for each role.

2. The product in one page

Pedi Clinic is a phone-first, installable web app (PWA) for one pediatric clinic: one pediatrician, about 20 children a day, and an in-house pharmacy. It replaces the paper token and waiting-room shouting.

  • Parents sign in with their phone number, add their children, check in to get a numbered token for today, and watch a live queue from their phone. The app notifies them when they're 3rd in line and when it's their turn. Afterwards they see a visit summary with the photographed prescription, the fee paid and any follow-up date. They can also book appointments (a session on a day up to a week ahead).
  • The doctor runs the day from a phone, tablet or front-desk laptop: calls children from the queue, photographs the handwritten prescription, records fees and how they were paid (cash, UPI, card or split), sets follow-ups, opens booking sessions and looks at analytics.
  • The pharmacist sees each completed visit instantly, reads the prescription photo, picks medicines and dispenses them (stock is deducted automatically), and manages stock.
  • The owner (the product team, not clinic staff) sees app rating, install and notification adoption, and app-versus-walk-in usage.

Who uses it, and where

Tone: calm, warm and reassuring, never clinical or alarming. Parents are anxious about a sick child; the queue screen should make waiting feel under control.

3. Design constraints the redesign must keep

Platform and layout

  • Mobile-first, not mobile-only. Every role must work well at 320–430 px wide with no sideways scrolling, and also on tablets and laptops.
  • Navigation adapts to width: a bottom tab bar on phones, a narrow icon rail on the left on tablets, and a full sidebar with the app name (and Sign out at the bottom) on laptops.
  • Sheets, not small dialogs: secondary tasks (add child, add walk-in, search, complete visit, dispense, child history) open as a full-screen sheet on phones and a right-hand drawer from tablet width up. Clicking outside or pressing Escape closes the drawer.
  • One primary action per screen, pinned to the bottom (for example "Check in", "Get token", "Book appointment") in a bottom dock that sits just above the tab bar on phones.
  • Card grids use 1 column on phones, 2 on tablets and 3 on wide screens.
  • Respect notches and home indicators (safe areas).

Touch and accessibility

  • Touch targets at least 48 px. Pinch-zoom is never disabled.
  • Status is never shown by colour alone; each status also has a text label.
  • Light theme by default; dark mode follows the phone's setting. Designs should include both.
  • Motion is subtle and fast, and turns off for people who choose reduced motion.
  • Toasts (short confirmations and errors) appear at the top centre.

Every screen needs these states

  • Loading: skeleton placeholders in the shape of the content (never a blank screen or a lone spinner).
  • Empty: an icon, a short title, one helpful sentence and usually one action (the copy for each screen is given below).
  • Error: "Couldn't load this" with the reason and a Try again button.
  • Offline: a slim "You're offline" banner; live screens keep showing the last data and catch up when the connection returns.
  • Success: a toast, for example "Visit completed for Aarav" or "Dispensed — ₹340".

The centrepiece

The parent's token screen ("Your queue") is the visual heart of the product: a very large token number, a clear status colour, "Now serving" and plain, reassuring copy. Design this screen first and give it the most care.

Real content, no fake data

All data comes from the clinic's database. Designs may use sample Indian names (for example Aarav, Diya, Kabir, Ananya), realistic ages (such as "2 years, 3 months, 5 days"), 10-digit Indian mobile numbers and ₹ amounts from ₹0 to about ₹2,000.

6. Parent screens

6.1 Sign in / (signed out)

Purpose: get a parent in with only their phone. There are no passwords.

  • Step 1: title "Pedi Clinic", subtitle "Check in for your child's visit and follow the queue from your phone.", a Mobile number field (number pad, 10 digits exactly; shows an error for 9 or 11 digits or letters), and a Send code button (disabled until valid).
  • Step 2: "Enter the 6-digit code we sent to +91…", a Verification code field (auto-fills from SMS), Verify, and "Use a different number".
  • States: sending or verifying (button spinner), wrong code or network error (inline under the field).
  • In a browser tab, the install popup (6.12) opens on this screen too, and the small install banner sits below the form. The installed app doesn't show either.

6.2 Your name first sign-in only

"What should we call you?", with the note "The doctor sees this alongside your child's details." It has one field (Your name) and a Continue button. If the doctor had already added this phone number as a walk-in, the parent's children and past visits are attached to their account automatically.

6.3 Home /

Purpose: the parent's base: which child is in today's queue, their children, and the way to check in.

Top to bottom

  1. Header: "Pedi Clinic", a notification bell with an unread count, and Sign out (icon on phones; in the sidebar wider up).
  2. Install / notifications card: see 6.12; only appears when relevant.
  3. Today's tokens (only if any child is in today's queue): one card per token with a square token-number tile, the child's name and a status pill. Tapping it opens Your queue. Updates live.
  4. Your children with an + Add child link. Each child card shows the name, exact age ("2 years, 3 months, 5 days"), a pencil to edit, and, while in the queue, "Token 7 · Vaccination" plus a "Currently in queue" label. Child cards have no check-in button of their own.
  5. Bottom dock (always pinned to the bottom): a "Know about your doctor" link; the "Enable notifications for live queue alerts [Turn on]" banner (only while undecided); and a wide coral Check in button (only when at least one child exists).

States

  • No children: empty state "No children added yet", "Add your child's name and date of birth to check in.", button Add your first child.
  • Loading skeleton; error with Try again.

6.4 Add child / Edit child sheet

  • Fields: Child's name and Date of birth (a date, not an age; can't be in the future). The hint shows the live result: "Age today: 3 years, 2 months, 4 days".
  • Button: Save child / Save changes (disabled until valid and changed).
  • Edit only: a delete area. "Added by mistake? Deleting removes Aarav and any upcoming bookings. A child who has already visited the clinic keeps their records and can't be deleted." It uses a two-tap confirm button: "Delete Aarav" → "Tap again to delete Aarav".

6.5 Check in /check-in

Purpose: get today's token, only inside the clinic: scan the QR at reception, then two choices, who and why.

  1. Scan the QR code at reception: the camera opens straight away, full width, with a scanning frame and "Point your camera at the QR code at reception. It scans by itself." Nothing else is shown until a scan succeeds. States: opening the camera; "That's not the clinic's check-in QR code"; camera access off (steps to allow it, Try again). After a scan: a green "QR code scanned — you're at the clinic" line above the choices.
  2. "Who is visiting?": selectable child cards. A child already in the queue is shown but disabled, labelled "Currently in queue". With one child available, it's pre-selected.
  3. "Reason for the visit": two large option cards, Vaccination and General checkup. There's no free-text field and no symptom list.
  4. Pinned coral button: Get token (disabled until both are chosen). On success, the app goes straight to Your queue.

Special case: the child has an upcoming booking

In place of the reason picker, a highlighted card asks: "Are you coming for the same reason you booked your upcoming appointment?" It gives the booking details (child, reason, day and time) and two buttons: Yes, same reason (gets the token immediately using that booking) and No, something else (shows the normal reason picker).

Rules: one active token per child at a time. If the scanned code is no longer the clinic's (the doctor replaced it), "That check-in code isn't valid any more. Scan the QR code at reception again." and the camera reopens. Errors show as a toast.

Printable check-in QR (staff) /admin/check-in-qr

A page that shows and prints the clinic's check-in QR as an A4 poster: clinic logo and name, "Scan to check in", a large QR code and two lines of instructions for parents. Buttons (not printed): Print, and for the doctor Replace with a new code (two taps; every old printout stops working). Opened from a QR icon in the doctor's Queue header.

6.6 Your queue: the token screen /queue (centrepiece)

Purpose: let the parent relax. The app tells them when to go in.

One big card per active token (a parent can have more than one child in the queue):

  • Coloured top half (its colour follows the status): "AARAV'S TOKEN", the giant token number, and the reason (Vaccination / General checkup).
  • Bottom half: a status pill, "Now serving 12", and one line of plain copy for the status:

Waitingteal"3 patients ahead of you." / when 0: "You're next — please stay close by."
Calledcoral"It's your turn now — please go in."
In consultationdeep teal"You're with the doctor now."

  • "Patients ahead" counts only waiting children with a lower token number. There is no estimated wait time, on purpose.
  • Below: the install / notifications card, and a small note: "This screen updates on its own — no need to refresh."
  • Empty: "No active token", "Check in to get today's token and follow the queue live. Summaries of finished visits are in Last visits.", with Check in and "Go to Last visits".
  • Updates live; after the phone sleeps or goes offline, it catches up by itself.

6.7 Visit summary /visits/[id]

Opens automatically on whatever screen the parent is on when the doctor completes the visit (not when they open the app later). It can also be opened from Last visits.

  • Card: child name, date and reason.
  • Total paid: ₹ amount, large.
  • Follow-up visit (if set): the date plus "we'll remind you beforehand."
  • Prescription: photo thumbnails (up to 3) with "Tap a photo to zoom, download, or share it." With no photo: "No prescription photo was added for this visit."
  • Rating (only if this parent has never rated): "How is this app working for you?" with 1–5 stars. Nothing else is asked, and it's asked once per parent, ever. Afterwards: "Thanks for rating the app".

Prescription viewer (full screen)

Black background, pinch-zoom and pan, Close, Download, and Share (the phone's share sheet, for example WhatsApp; only shown where the phone supports it). With several photos, the parent picks from thumbnails.

6.8 Last visits /records (tab)

  • Title "Last visits", subtitle "Every finished visit, with the doctor's notes and prescriptions."
  • Filter chips (scroll sideways): Child (All children / each child; only shown with 2+ children) and Reason (All reasons / Vaccination / General checkup), with a count such as "4 visits match".
  • Visit cards, newest first: the child's initial in a circle, name, date, ₹ fee, a reason chip with an icon (syringe / stethoscope), a coral "Follow-up 12 Oct" chip if set, prescription thumbnails, and a View visit summary › row.
  • Empty: "No visits yet". Filtered to nothing: "No visits match", with Clear filters.

6.9 Appointments /appointments (tab)

  • Explainer under the title: "Booking a session doesn't skip the queue — check in when you arrive and you'll get a token as usual. Bookings can't be changed once made."
  • Cards: child name, day and session (for example "Wed, 1 Oct · Morning (10 am – 1 pm)"), reason, and a status pill (Booked / Arrived / Missed / Cancelled).
  • No cancel or reschedule buttons: only the clinic can cancel.
  • Pinned coral button: Book an appointment.
  • Empty: "No upcoming appointments", "Book a session that suits you, up to a week ahead."

6.10 Book an appointment /appointments/book

Deliberately feels like check-in: who → why → when.

  1. "Who is the appointment for?": child cards.
  2. "Reason for the visit": Vaccination / General checkup.
  3. When: pick a day (only days in the next 7 with an open session are shown), then a session (for example "Morning (10 am – 1 pm)", "Evening (6 pm – 9 pm)" or a custom time). A day where this child already has a booking shows "Already booked" and can't be picked. Sessions show no capacity or slot counts, and several children can book the same session.
  4. Pinned coral button: Book appointment. The booking is confirmed straight away (there's no approval step), with a toast such as "Booked for Wed, 1 Oct, Morning".
  • If the doctor closes a session while the parent is looking at it: "That session was just closed. Please pick another."
  • No open sessions: "The doctor hasn't opened any sessions in the booking window yet. Please check again later, or come in and take a token."
  • No children: "Add your child first".

6.11 Know about your doctor /doctor

A profile page in a fixed order: name → portrait photo → title and years of experience → clinic location → qualifications and languages spoken → areas of expertise → professional highlights. Sections without data are hidden. Empty: "Doctor's profile coming soon".

6.12 Install, notifications and system overlays

Install popup (every visit in a browser tab)

  • "Install Pedi Clinic". On iPhone Safari it shows steps (Share → Add to Home Screen); on Android Chrome it shows an Install app button.
  • Opened inside WhatsApp or another in-app browser: "Open this in your browser first", with Copy link.
  • "Not now" hides it for this visit, leaving a small banner. It shows again after getting a token. It depends only on how the app was opened, not on the account: every browser tab shows it; the app opened from the home screen never does.
  • Right after installing from the browser, a full-screen cover says: "App installed successfully! Please close this browser tab and open Pedi Clinic directly from your phone's home screen to continue." It has no close button.

Notification permission (in the installed app only)

  • A soft modal, at most once per visit, at helpful moments:
    • On Home: "Get live alerts when your turn is approaching"
    • After getting a token: "Want an alert when it's almost your turn?"
    • After booking: "Get reminded about this appointment"
    Each has a short body, Turn on and Not now.
  • After "Not now", a compact banner stays: "Enable notifications for live queue alerts [Turn on]" (in Home's bottom dock; at the top of other screens).
  • If blocked: "Notifications are blocked", with phone-specific steps to re-enable (iPhone Settings → Notifications → Pedi Clinic; Android Settings → Apps → Pedi Clinic → Notifications).
  • Once allowed or blocked, the modal and banner disappear for good.

Notifications list (bell)

A sheet with the newest first: title, one-line body and time. Unread items are highlighted, each has a delete (bin) button, and tapping one goes to the relevant screen. Empty: "Nothing yet", "We'll let you know here when you're 3rd in line and when it's your turn." Everything that is pushed also lands here.

7. Doctor screens

7.1 Staff sign in /admin/login

"Staff sign in", with Email, Password and Sign in. The same screen serves the doctor and the pharmacist; the role decides where they land (Queue or Pharmacy feed).

7.2 Queue /admin/queue (home tab)

Purpose: run the day. The list updates live as parents check in.

Header: "Queue", with 🔍 Search children and ＋ Add walk-in icon buttons.

Each queue card

  • A token number tile; the child's name; exact age · reason.
  • Pills: status, New or Returning, and an Appointment badge if they booked.
  • Tapping the top of the card opens the Child history sheet.
  • Actions depend on the status:

In consultationComplete visit (coral; opens the wizard)Skip · Remove

Empty: "No one in the queue", "Tokens appear here the moment a parent checks in.", Add walk-in.

7.3 Add walk-in sheet

For parents without the app. Fields: Child's name, Date of birth, Parent's mobile number (10 digits), and Reason for the visit (Vaccination / General checkup). The note says: "The visit links to this number and appears in their app when they sign in." On success: "Token 14 created for Diya".

7.4 Search children sheet

A single field, Name or phone number, with live results (child, age, parent, phone). Tapping a result opens Child history. No match: "No matches", "Try a different spelling, or search by the parent's phone number."

7.5 Child history sheet

The header shows the child's name, age, parent's name and a tap-to-call phone. Then the last visit summary first, then the full history: date, reason, fee and prescription photos. New child: "First visit" / "No visits yet".

7.6 Complete visit: 5-step wizard sheet, one step per screen

  1. Prescription: Take photo opens the rear camera. Up to 3 photos ("Add another page"), each with a retake button; photos are compressed on the phone. Button: Skip for now (no photos) or Next.
  2. Fees: three ₹ fields, Consultation, Vaccination and Other (any may be 0), with a live Amount due total. There are no preset or default fees.
  3. Payment (skipped when the total is ₹0): choose Cash, UPI, Card or Hybrid. Hybrid asks for an amount per mode, which must add up exactly to the total ("Entered ₹… of ₹…").
  4. Follow-up (optional): a future date, with a Clear button. It only schedules a reminder for the parent; it doesn't create an appointment.
  5. Review: child, prescription count, each fee, each payment mode (or "No payment due") and the follow-up date, with a coral Complete visit button.

Back buttons on every step. Closing with anything entered asks: "Are you sure? Unsaved data will be lost.", with Keep editing / Discard and close. On complete, the visit appears instantly in the pharmacy feed, the parent's phone jumps to the summary, and the toast says "Visit completed for Aarav".

Only one child can be in consultation at a time. Calling another child while one is open asks the doctor to finish the current one first.

7.7 Appointments /admin/appointments

  • Today and upcoming days, grouped by day, then by session.
  • Each session shows a summary line, "3 booked · 2 vaccination, 1 general checkup", then each booking: child name, reason, age, parent name and a tap-to-call phone number.
  • Actions: Cancel session and Cancel day, both two-tap ("Tap again — parents will be told"). Affected parents are notified immediately. There are no approve/reject buttons.
  • Empty: "No sessions scheduled", "Open a session from the Availability tab." A session with no bookings: "No bookings yet".

7.8 History /admin/history

  • "Patient history": only children whose consultation was completed.
  • Defaults to today (updates live). Use ‹ › to step a day, or a date box to jump to any past date.
  • A Reason filter, and a search box, "Search child, parent or phone", that searches across all days.
  • Rows: child, parent (or "Parent's name not given"), phone, and "General checkup · 3 visits" (last reason and visit count).
  • Tapping a row opens the patient timeline (sheet): every consultation, newest first, with date, token, reason, a booked-appointment tag, minutes with the doctor, fees and how they were paid, follow-up, medicines dispensed (or "Not collected yet" / "Bought elsewhere"), and View prescription.
  • Empty: "No consultations yet today" / "No one found" / "No one matches".

7.9 Notification /admin/notifications

The doctor's inbox, newest first. Mainly "New appointment: Aarav" with the reason and session, plus a "See all bookings" link to Appointments. Unread items stay highlighted for this visit, and each can be deleted. Empty: "No notifications", "When a parent books or moves an appointment, it shows up here."

7.10 Analytics /admin/analytics (doctor only)

Subtitle "Only you can see these numbers." A segmented switch toggles End of day and Trends. Every chart has a legend and a Table toggle to show the numbers, and tapping a column shows its value.

End of day (pick a date, defaults to today)

  • Stat tiles: Children seen, Total collected (consultations + pharmacy), Visit fees, Pharmacy sales (with order count), Appointments arrived, Appointments missed.
  • Fee tiles: Consultation / Vaccination / Other fees.
  • Split bars: booked appointments attended vs missed; consultations by payment mode (Cash / UPI / Card); consultations by fee type.

Trends (range: today / 7 days / 30 days / custom; group by day, week or month)

  • Tiles: Children seen, Total collected, Visit fees, Pharmacy sales, Average consultation (minutes), Skipped or removed (x of y tokens), Appointments kept, Follow-ups returned.
  • Column charts: children seen per day, week or month; consultation revenue split by fee type or payment mode; pharmacy sales; booked appointments attended vs missed.
  • Split bars: reason for visit; new vs returning; how they joined the queue (same-day token vs booked appointment).
  • Busiest check-in hours: tokens taken by hour of the day.
  • Empty: "Nothing in this range yet".
Analytics must not show app ratings or install and notification adoption; those belong to the Owner only.

7.11 Availability /admin/availability

Purpose: open the sessions parents can book. Everything else stays open for walk-ins.

  • Pick a date.
  • One-tap presets: "Morning (10 am – 1 pm)" and "Evening (6 pm – 9 pm)" ("One tap adds it straight away."). A preset already open that day shows as added.
  • Or a custom time: From and To boxes (number pad; typing "5" or "5:30" works) with an AM/PM toggle beside each, and a confirmation such as "Reads as 5:30 PM". An end time before the start shows "Must be after the start". There are no dropdowns and no slot counts.
  • Helpers: Copy last week and Mark day closed (two-tap: "Tap again — booked parents will be told").
  • The session cards for that day list every booked child with their reason, parent name and a tap-to-call number, plus Cancel session.
  • Empty: "No sessions", "Nothing open on Wed, 1 Oct yet — use a button above."

8. Pharmacist screens

8.1 Pharmacy feed /admin/feed (home tab)

  • A live list of completed visits waiting for medicines. A new one appears the moment the doctor completes a visit.
  • Card: token number tile, child name, age · reason, and "2 prescription photos" (or "No prescription photo"). Tapping it opens Dispense.
  • Empty: "Nothing waiting", "Visits appear here the moment the doctor completes them."

8.2 Dispense sheet

  • Title: "Token 7 · Aarav".
  • Prescription photo(s) at the top; they open full screen with pinch-zoom and pan.
  • Find a medicine: search as you type. Each result shows name, unit, price and "12 left", with − / + quantity buttons (capped at the stock available).
  • Footer: item count and a running ₹ total, a Dispense button (deducts stock in one safe step and is refused if stock ran out), and "Buying elsewhere — skip" (clears the visit from the feed).
  • Toasts: "Dispensed — ₹340" / "Token 7 cleared".

8.3 Stock /admin/stock

  • Search medicines and filter chips with counts: All · In stock · Low stock · Out of stock. Out-of-stock and low items sort to the top.
  • A low-stock warning summary at the top when anything is low.
  • Rows: name, unit, price per unit, quantity, a low or out badge, and a Restock button.
  • Add medicine (sheet): Name, Unit ("tablet, ml, strip"), Price per unit, Quantity in stock, and Warn below ("You'll see a low-stock warning at or below this quantity.").
  • Restock (sheet): Quantity received → "Paracetamol syrup now at 40".
  • Empty: "No medicines yet", "Add what the pharmacy carries so visits can be dispensed."

The pharmacist cannot see fees, payments or analytics.

9. Owner screens

9.1 Team sign in /owner/login

"Team sign in", with Email and Password. Note: "This sign-in is for the Pedi Clinic team only. Clinic staff sign in at /admin." Doctor and pharmacist accounts are refused here.

9.2 Owner dashboard /owner

A single page that updates live:

11. End-to-end flows

A. A typical visit (the demo path)

  1. Parent opens the link → signs in with a phone number and code → enters their name → adds a child (name + date of birth).
  2. Taps Check in → picks the child → picks General checkup → Get token → lands on Your queue: "Token 7 · 3 patients ahead of you". The notification prompt appears.
  3. The queue moves; the parent gets a "3rd in line" push.
  4. Doctor (Queue tab) taps Call on token 7 → the parent's card turns coral: "It's your turn now — please go in." → the doctor taps Start consultation.
  5. The doctor taps Complete visit → photographs the prescription → fees ₹500 consultation → Hybrid: ₹300 UPI + ₹200 cash → follow-up in 2 weeks → Complete visit.
  6. The parent's phone jumps to the Visit summary: prescription, ₹500 paid, follow-up date → they rate the app 5★.
  7. Pharmacist: token 7 appears in the feed → opens it → zooms into the prescription → adds medicines → Dispense ("Dispensed — ₹340").
  8. Owner dashboard: the rating and usage update live.

B. Booking ahead

  1. Doctor → Availability → picks Thursday → taps "Morning (10 am – 1 pm)".
  2. Parent → Appointments → Book an appointment → child → Vaccination → Thursday → Morning → Book appointment → "Booked".
  3. Doctor gets "New appointment: Diya" in Notification; it appears under Thursday Morning in Appointments.
  4. Wednesday 7 pm: "Appointment tomorrow". Thursday 8 am: "Appointment today".
  5. Thursday: the parent taps Check in → gets a token as usual; the doctor's card shows the Appointment badge. Queue order is still first-come, first-served.
  6. If they never arrive, the booking shows as Missed the next morning.

C. Walk-in without the app

  1. Doctor → Queue → ＋ Add walk-in → name, date of birth, parent's mobile, reason → a token is created.
  2. Later the parent installs the app and signs in with the same number: the child and the visit are already there.

D. Doctor cancels a day

  1. Doctor → Availability (or Appointments) → Mark day closed / Cancel day → "Tap again — booked parents will be told".
  2. Every booked parent gets "Your appointment has been cancelled."; their booking shows Cancelled.

12. Do not add: things the app deliberately doesn't have

These were considered and left out on purpose. A design that includes them is off-brief.

Parent side

  • Estimated wait time or ETA (only "patients ahead")
  • Cancel, reschedule or edit a booking (only the clinic can cancel)
  • Leaving the queue by themselves
  • Symptom or complaint fields, free-text notes to the doctor
  • Choosing a time slot inside a session, or seeing slot counts
  • Online payment or payment in the app
  • Rating the doctor, written reviews, feedback forms, Google review links
  • Chat, calls or messaging with the clinic
  • Vaccination charts, growth charts, health records beyond visits
  • AI summaries, OCR of prescriptions
  • Multiple clinics, multiple doctors, clinic search or maps
  • Regional languages or a language switcher
  • Social login or passwords for parents
  • Profile photos for children

Staff side

  • Approving or rejecting bookings (bookings are auto-confirmed)
  • Session capacity or slot limits (sessions have no capacity)
  • Urgent or priority flags that jump the queue
  • Default or preset fee amounts
  • Typing medicines at the consultation (the prescription is a photo; the pharmacist picks medicines)
  • A clinic open or closed status banner
  • Ratings or app adoption in the doctor's analytics
  • Staff management, roles or settings screens
  • Supplier, purchase-order or expiry tracking in stock
  • Printing, invoices or GST bills

Not built yet (don't design unless asked)

  • Privacy notice / parental consent screen (needed before launch)
  • Push notifications for the doctor (in-app inbox only today)
